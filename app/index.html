    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough — PowerPyx rates the platinum about 4/10 and roughly 200 hours, long rather than hard. Work the five stages in order; the gold for 100% completion is the last thing to chase, not the first.

      Before you start: the save rule

      GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus (PowerPyx; Output Lag). Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. That single habit is what makes the one missable trophy safe.

      Missables & saves

      Built from the official descriptions and the launch guides that checked them: only one trophy on this list can actually be missed. Everything else is either automatic story progress or free-roam content you can return to.

      Story trophies are not missable — but they are one-shot

      Every chapter and named-mission trophy unlocks automatically when you finish that mission, and PowerPyx lists them as unmissable. The catch is the missing replay feature: if you want to redo a mission, only a manual save lets you. That is why the roadmap asks for a save at each chapter start, not because the trophies themselves can be missed.

      Save plan

      • Start of Chapter 1 — before anything else; the Chapter 1 meditation lives here.
      • Start of Chapters 2, 3 and 4 — one save each, kept separate.
      • Before the final mission — a spare save at the point of no return.
      • After the finale — a clean post-story save for the collectible and activity stages.

      Sources & method

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list, as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles and checked against each other before this guide was written. Counts (such as 250 stunts or 18 meditation spots) are PSN's tracked progress targets from PSNProfiles.

        How the guides were written

        Each guide is written in this guide's own words. Where a method was confirmed by a launch guide, the guide says so and names it; where launch guides had not confirmed a method (five trophies at launch, per PowerPyx), the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location or a step. A guide that only repeats the trophy's description earns nothing — every entry here adds what to do, where, and when it can be missed, with its source beside it.

        What this guide is not

        It uses no Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshots, and it fetches nothing from the network: every file it needs ships with it, so it works with the network off. It is an independent fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games, PlayStation, Exophase, PSNProfiles or PowerPyx.
